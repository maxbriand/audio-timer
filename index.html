      Phone use

      Android's own usage log, read back: every screen-on, every unlock, every app in front. Times only — never what was on the screen, and never why it woke (button, lift or notification look the same).

      Usage access needed

      Android keeps this log by itself, for every app; reading it takes one switch that only you can flip. Turn on Audio Timer in the list, then come back — the days already in the log show up at once.

      Switch greyed out, or a "Restricted setting" message? Android does that to apps installed from an APK: open App info → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings, then ask again.
